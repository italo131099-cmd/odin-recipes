Quick 20 minutes rice recipe!

Description

This is the perfect recipe for a simple and quick to make side dish that is decilious and goes well with literally anything!From veggies, to any type of meat, rice is the perfect side dish that will make anyone happy! It only takes five ingredients and the total prep time is 25 minutes at most.

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Garlic clove
  • Onion
  • Olive oil or butter
  • Salt
  • Cilantro (optional)

Steps

  1. Chop the garlic and the onion as thin as you like. I personally like them super thin so they can be distributed more uniformly
  2. Add olive oil to a cooking pot on medium heat until the pot gets hot
  3. Sautee the onions until they are light brown and all the water from them has evaporated
  4. Then add the garlic and sautee it until it gets golden brown
  5. Add the rice and mix it for a minute
  6. Add the water, decrease the heat, and cook it for 18 minutes
  7. Turn of the heat after the timer goes off, slightly open the lid and let it rest for 5 minutes
  8. Chop some cilantro and add on top of it if you are a cilantro fan
  9. Enjoy it!
